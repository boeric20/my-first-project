# Week 1 — Rebuild Base Phase
**Dates:** Mon Aug 3 – Sun Aug 9, 2026
**Phase:** Base (Rebuild Week 1 of ~12)
**Planned running volume:** ~90 min easy running (roughly 8–8.5 mi). No prior week to compare against; this is the baseline everything ramps from.

## Where this sits vs. your reference programs
Your TTM run blocks show trained levels around 25–30 miles per week with a 8-mile long run, mile repeats at ~8:10/mi, and tempo work. That is the target, not the starting line. After 6 months off with only light movement, we start at roughly a third of that volume and zero hard intensity. If the next few weeks go well, expect about 10–12 weeks to reach reference volume, and a bit longer to hit reference paces. We add ~10% running volume per week and take a lighter deload every 3rd or 4th week.

## Two coaching cues this week
1. **Easy means easy.** Every run this week is conversational Z2. If you can't talk in full sentences, walk until your heart rate drops, then resume. Pushing easy runs just buys fatigue with no benefit (straight from the TTM run guidance).
2. **Lift for quality, not load.** Leave 3–4 reps in the tank on every set. We are waking up tendons, joints, and movement patterns, not testing strength. Grinding reps this week is the wrong trade.

## Why the structure looks like this
I capped running at 3 days and added optional bike Z2 on off days. Reason: after a 6-month layoff your aerobic fitness comes back faster than bones and tendons tolerate impact. Keeping run frequency low while padding easy aerobic volume on the bike builds the base without overloading tissue that hasn't run in half a year. This is my call based on the ramp rules, not a specific Kevin prescription.

**Newsletter input:** Kevin's two most recent newsletters (Jul 24 and Jul 29) were podcast-promo emails with no written coaching to apply. One recurring theme was carb intake on harder training days. Week 1 has no hard sessions, so fuel normally. We'll revisit carb timing when intensity comes in around Week 3.

## Monday — Easy Run + Strides
- 25 min easy run, Z2, conversational pace. Off-pavement if you can.
- Then 4 x 15 sec strides on flat ground or a slight incline. Build to a smooth fast-but-relaxed pace, hold, ease down. Full recovery (~45–60 sec) between each. These are not sprints.
- *Why: shortest run of the week on purpose. First run back should feel easy. Strides reintroduce turnover at almost no fatigue cost.*

## Tuesday — Lift A (lower-biased full body)
Lower warmup first (~8–10 min): easy bike or backward walk, goblet squat with pause x8, single-leg RDL reach x8/leg, pogo hops 20 sec, world's greatest stretch x2/side.
- Goblet or barbell back squat — 3 x 8 (RIR 3–4)
- Romanian deadlift (BB, hex bar, or DBs) — 3 x 8 (RIR 3–4)
- Split squat or leg press — 2 x 10 / leg
- Standing or seated calf raise — 2 x 12–15
- Side plank — 2 x 30 sec / side
- Dead bug — 2 x 10 / side
- *Why: moderate loads, higher reps-in-reserve than the reference program (which runs RIR 1–2). That gap is intentional for a return week. My recommendation, not Kevin's.*

## Wednesday — Easy Run
- 30 min easy run, Z2, conversational. No strides today.
- *Why: pure aerobic volume. Keep it boring and easy.*

## Thursday — Lift B (upper-biased full body)
Upper warmup first (~8–10 min): dead hang 30 sec, pushup-plus x6–8, band pull-apart x12–15, light overhead DB carry, PVC Y-W.
- DB or barbell bench press — 3 x 8 (RIR 3–4)
- Chest-supported row — 3 x 10 (RIR 3)
- Lat pulldown or assisted chin-up — 2 x 10
- DB shoulder press — 2 x 10
- Cable face pull — 2 x 15
- Hanging knee raise or plank — 2 x 30–40 sec
- *Why: full upper reintroduction, same quality-over-load rule.*

## Friday — Rest (optional Z2 bike)
- Full rest, or 45 min easy Z2 on the stationary bike if you feel good. The bike is recovery, not a workout. Keep it in low Z2.
- *Why: space between the two lift days and the weekend run. Bike is free aerobic base with no impact.*

## Saturday — Longer Easy Run + Strides
- 35 min easy run, Z2. This is the week's aerobic anchor, not a real long run yet.
- Then 4 x 20 sec strides, flat or slight incline, full recovery between.
- *Why: slightly longer continuous effort to start rebuilding durability. Still fully easy.*

## Sunday — Rest (optional Z2 bike + walk)
- Full rest, or 45 min easy Z2 bike plus a walk to hit 8–10k steps.
- *Why: recover for Week 2. Steps and easy spinning keep the aerobic system ticking without cost.*

### Flags to watch
- Any sharp, localized, or lingering pain gets logged with a Y in the Pain column, and we scale back next week. Normal muscle soreness is fine and expected.
- If a 25–35 min continuous run spikes your heart rate out of Z2, switch to run/walk intervals to hold Z2. That is not a setback, it is the correct move.
- Log every session the same day in `Training-Log.xlsx` (runs and cardio on one tab, lifts on the other). I read both before building Week 2.
